Frequently Asked Questions

Who is ADR Quest for?

It is designed for medical, pharmacy, and nursing students, clinical staff, and patients wanting to learn about drug safety, pharmacovigilance, and adverse reaction report procedures.

Is there a certificate awarded?

Yes, completing all 8 learning modules and getting a passing grade on the final evaluations unlocks a downloadable, print-friendly clinical certificate of completion.
